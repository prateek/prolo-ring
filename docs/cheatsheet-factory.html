Modstrip

Count the taps, then hold. Tap the Modstrip the given number of times, then press and hold within two seconds. Any trackpad touch cancels. The LED bar previews the count.

Reading the LEDs

  • Mode flashLED 1, 2, or 3 twice; all three for Air
  • Tap previewbar grows 1→2→3, then brighter 1→2→3 for taps 4 to 6
  • Acceptedthe preview bar flashes three times
  • Cancelledsweep down 3→2→1, then dark
  • Pairingall three breathe, then a dim pulse every 5 s
  • Connectedslot bar, then all three solid, then fade
  • Chargingbattery bar breathes for 20 s; three solid bars is full
  • Low batterythree fast blinks, then it powers off

When it misbehaves

  • Nothing lightscable on a 5 V wall charger for 10 min; rub the contacts
  • Won't pairforget it on the host, then 2× tap + hold
  • Pointer deadtap the Modstrip to wake; swipe left; 1× tap + hold unlocks
  • Studio can't see it3× tap + hold for App Status; 2× to re-advertise
  • Lock itpalm over the surface; the charger or 1× tap + hold unlocks
Light touch: the surface is sensitive, glide rather than press.Dimmed gestures are switched off in this profile; change that in Prolo Studio.
