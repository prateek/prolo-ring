Modstrip tap ladder

Tap the Modstrip the given number of times, then press and hold within two seconds. Any trackpad touch cancels. The LED bar previews the count.

  • 1× tap + hold Reconnect, switch to the next remembered host, or unlock
    one: back on
  • 2× tap + hold Open a 60-second pairing window
    two: new
  • 3× tap + hold App Status on or off (reboots; mouse input stops while in App Status)
    three: Studio
  • 4× tap + hold careful Shut down; only the charger wakes it
    four: lights out
  • 5× tap + hold careful Factory reset: clears bonds, settings, and profile
    five: wipe it all
  • 6× tap + hold careful Firmware update mode
    six: fix
  • 7× tap + hold careful Desktop or mobile navigation style; then forget the ring on the host and pair again
    seven: flip
  • Palm hold Lock input until 1× tap + hold or the charger
    Cover the touch surface with the palm

Worth knowing

  • A recognized gesture ticks the mode's LED once; that tick is your confirmation.
  • The ring starts in the mode it was last in.
  • Hold left button latches a drag; a single tap releases it.
  • Tap + hold temporary pointer needs Multi-tap recognition on in Studio.
  • Chords marked Windows were authored for Windows; ⌘ is what a Mac receives.
  • In App Status the ring is not a mouse; 3× tap + hold brings it back.
